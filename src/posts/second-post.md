---
title: 'How React Made Me More Confident'
date: 'November 15, 2025'
excerpt: 'Before React, I could build things, but I wasnt always sure I understood what I was building. React changed that. Thinking in components forced me to break problems down intentionally, and that skill carried over into everything else I code.'
---

Learning vanilla JavaScript was discouraging for me. I understood the concepts, like functions, loops, and scope, but applying that knowledge to real-world problems was a constant struggle. It felt like I was always hitting a wall. At one point, I seriously wondered if I just wasn't cut out to be a developer because of how much I was struggling.

Then I started learning React, and everything changed for me. For the first time, I actually felt like I was retaining information, not just memorizing syntax, but genuinely understanding how to apply it and carrying that understanding into projects outside of school. That was a new experience for me.

Part of my success with React stemmed from the solid foundation I already had in HTML. React is built on that foundation in a way that makes sense to me. The component-based structure felt familiar, which meant I wasn't starting from scratch; I was simply extending my existing knowledge.

The sense of knowing what I was doing motivated me to keep going. Where JavaScript had made me doubt my path, React reminded me why I chose to pursue development in the first place.

If you're early in your journey and vanilla JavaScript has you feeling stuck, don't interpret that as a sign you don't belong in this field. Sometimes, you just need the right entry point. For me, that entry point was React.
